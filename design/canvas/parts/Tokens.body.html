Design system

Spatial Solid

Layered depth with opaque surfaces. Elevation comes from a lightness step, a hairline border and a single top highlight — never from transparency, so dense data stays legible at every level.

Surface ladder

Surfaces & lines

Four opaque steps. Each level adds roughly 0.04 lightness, one hairline, and one 7% inner top highlight.
